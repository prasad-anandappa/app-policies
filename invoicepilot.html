Privacy Policy — InvoicePilot

Data stored on your device

InvoicePilot stores your invoices, client details and business profile in the app's private storage on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers by the mobile app.

Purchases

Pro purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment-card details. Purchase records are handled under Google Play's own privacy policy.

Data collected by us

The mobile app collects no analytics and contains no advertising SDKs.

Data shared with third parties

None, other than Google Play processing purchases you choose to make.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all on-device invoice data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
