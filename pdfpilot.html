Privacy Policy — PDFPilot Pro

Effective date: 3 November 2026 Developer: Prasad Dimba Anandappa (prasad.d.anandappa@gmail.com)

PDFPilot Pro is designed to work entirely on your device.

Data stored on your device

A list of recently opened files, daily usage counts, and any signatures you draw for placing on documents. The PDF files you open are processed locally and are not copied anywhere. This data never leaves your device. The app makes no network calls, has no user accounts, and includes no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data collected by us

None. We operate no server that receives your data, and we cannot access the data stored in the app on your device.

Data shared with third parties

None.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all of its on-device data. Where the app offers an in-app delete / delete-all function, that removes the data immediately as well.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted at the same address as this policy with a new effective date.
